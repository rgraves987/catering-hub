How the math works 


food sales     = price × (adults + kids × kids%)
revenue        = food sales × (1 + service%) + delivery fee
sales tax      = revenue × tax%            (collected for the state, not your money)
customer total = revenue + sales tax
card fees      = customer total × fee%
costs          = food + labor + fuel + travel + packaging × all guests + other
profit         = revenue − card fees − costs
margin         = profit ÷ revenue

price for margin m: revenue needed = costs ÷ (1 − fee% × (1 + tax%) − m)
                    price = (revenue needed − delivery) ÷ (1 + service%) ÷ (adults + kids × kids%)

Sales tax is applied to the whole bill, including delivery and service charges. Many states tax catering, and some tax delivery and service charges as well. Rules differ by state and city, so confirm with your state revenue department.

Quick guide: tax records
  • Pick a tax year. Income counts payments you recorded on each job's Payments tab, by payment date. Expenses count the costs of jobs whose event date is in that year.
  • Sales tax you collected is taken out of income. It belongs to the state.
  • Each expense shows the Schedule C line it usually goes on.
  • The amounts come from what you entered in each job. Update jobs with what you actually spent, and keep your receipts.
  • Download tax summary gives your preparer the totals. Job-by-job detail lists every job. Both open in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • This is a record-keeping aid, not tax advice. Check categories with your tax preparer.

Quick guide: paid access (only you see this tab)

New customer

  • Invite them in Netlify (Identity → Invite users).
  • Their free trial (3 days unless you change it below) starts the first time they sign in. You get a "New trial" notification with their email.
  • When they pay (online, or you press Record payment), the trial ends and paid access starts.
  • When a trial runs out, they see a "trial ended" lock screen with your Subscribe button. Each email gets only one trial.

Renewals and lockouts

  • + days adds a payment. If they're still active, the days are added to their current end date, so they never lose days for paying early.
  • When the date passes, they're locked out at the start of the next day (your time zone). Their jobs stay saved and come back when you record a payment.
  • Customers see a reminder 5 days before their access ends.
  • Lock now ends access today. Remove deletes their access record (to remove the login, delete them in Netlify Identity too).

Payment notifications

Online payments renew access automatically and show up here with the customer's email. New ones are highlighted.

Quick guide: how Setup works

Menu items

  • Rename any meat, side, extra or sauce, and change how much it makes (yield, package size, scoop, lb per quart, pack or bottle size).
  • Add: pick the type, enter a name and price, press Add item, then fill in its amounts in the table.
  • ↑ ↓ reorders items. Delete needs two clicks and tells you how many saved jobs use the item.
  • On for new jobs decides whether an item starts checked on a new job.
  • Prices are set on the My prices tab.

Portions and calculations

  • How much kids eat and what you call them, extra meat for 2+ meats, side scoop scaling, purchase rounding, old-price flag, food-cost warning and target range.

Dropdown choices

  • Edit, add or remove appetite levels, serving sizes, pan sizes and payment methods (one per line).

Defaults for new jobs

  • Business name, contact, tax, deposit, labor rate, prices and notes that every new job starts with.
  • Use the open job's values copies the current job in as your defaults.
  • One button puts your business name and contact on all saved jobs.

Jobs you already saved

  • Item details (yield, scoop size…) change amounts in saved jobs too. Each job keeps its own prices.
  • Items you add later start off in older jobs.
  • If you change a dropdown choice, a saved job keeps its old value, shown as "Earlier setting", until you pick a new one.
  • Everything saves to your account. Sign in on any device to see the same jobs and prices.
  • Download backup (under More) saves a copy of jobs, prices, menu items and settings.
